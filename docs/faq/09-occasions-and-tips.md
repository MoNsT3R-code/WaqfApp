# Ramadan, Eid and Special Occasions

## How can WaqfApp help me plan my finances for Ramadan?

Ramadan usually brings extra spending on suhoor and iftar food, hosting family, charity and Eid preparations, as well as the time many people choose to pay their Zakat. A few weeks before Ramadan, estimate these costs, set aside the amount from your income, and pay your utility bills early so that they do not add pressure during the month. During Ramadan, check your Payment History weekly to keep Food spending in line with your plan, and record cash spending with Add Cash.

## Why do many people give more charity in Ramadan?

Ramadan is a month in which good deeds are especially encouraged. Narrations describe the Prophet, peace be upon him, as the most generous of people, and most generous of all in Ramadan. Many Muslims therefore choose to give extra Sadaqah, pay their annual Zakat, sponsor iftar meals for others, or support long-term projects during the month, particularly in the last ten nights, which include Laylat al-Qadr.

## What is Fitrana (Zakat al-Fitr)?

Fitrana, also called Zakat al-Fitr or Sadaqat al-Fitr, is a small charitable payment due on behalf of each member of a household, including children, before the Eid al-Fitr prayer. Its purpose is to purify the fast and to make sure that people in need can also celebrate Eid. The amount is based on the value of a set measure of staple food; in Pakistan, religious authorities announce recommended amounts each year according to wheat, barley, dates and raisins. Check the current year's announcement before paying.

## When should Fitrana be paid?

Fitrana should be paid before the Eid al-Fitr prayer so that it reaches recipients in time for them to use it for Eid. Many scholars permit paying it during Ramadan, a day or more in advance, which is helpful when giving through organisations that need time to distribute it. Paying it after the Eid prayer without a valid reason is generally discouraged; it then counts as ordinary charity.

## How can I budget for Eid gifts and Eidi?

Make a list of everyone you plan to give Eidi or gifts to, decide an amount for each, and add up the total well before Eid. Withdraw or set aside that amount in advance so that you are not tempted to overspend in the busy last days. New banknotes for Eidi are popular in Pakistan, and banks often run special exchange counters before Eid, so plan ahead if you want them.

## How do I plan for Qurbani at Eid al-Adha?

Qurbani, the sacrifice of an animal at Eid al-Adha, is a significant expense for many families. Decide early whether you will buy an animal yourself, share in a larger animal such as a cow or camel (which can be shared by up to seven people), or pay an organisation to perform the sacrifice on your behalf, possibly in another region where the meat is needed more. Animal prices in Pakistan typically rise sharply as Eid approaches, so saving monthly and buying or booking earlier usually costs less.

## Can I use WaqfApp to send Eidi to relatives far away?

Yes. Use Send Money to send money to a relative's JazzCash or Easypaisa wallet or to their bank account, and Mobile Load to send phone credit. In the demonstration version no real money moves, but in a live service this would be a quick way to include relatives in other cities in your Eid giving. Send a little before Eid to allow for any delays during the holiday rush.

## How should I plan for Hajj compared with Umrah?

Hajj takes place only on specific days of the Islamic month of Dhul Hijjah, and in Pakistan places are allocated through the government Hajj scheme and through licensed private operators, with applications usually opening several months in advance. Costs are considerably higher than for Umrah and must normally be paid in full well before departure. WaqfApp's travel marketplace currently offers Umrah-style packages and individual hotels and flights, not Hajj packages.

## Is it permissible to borrow money to perform Umrah?

Scholars generally advise that Umrah, being highly recommended but not obligatory in the same way as Hajj for those able, should be performed with money one already has, and that it is better not to take on debt for it, especially interest-based debt. If you are considering borrowing for any religious journey, consult a scholar. Saving steadily towards the trip is the most widely recommended approach.

## How can I support others during Ramadan and Eid through the app?

You can pay a utility bill for a family facing hardship, send money to a relative or neighbour in need, top up the phone of a student away from home, or book an iftar meal through Order Food for someone who cannot afford it. Each of these is recorded in your Payment History, which helps you keep track of your giving alongside your other spending.

# Getting the Most from the Assistant

## What kinds of questions can I ask the Help Center assistant?

You can ask about anything covered in this FAQ: how to use each feature, such as sending money, paying bills, booking travel or using the Portfolio; what the app's terms and labels mean; the Islamic concepts behind WaqfApp, such as Waqf, Sadaqah Jariyah, Zakat and Qard al-Hasan; budgeting tips; security and privacy; troubleshooting; and, for developers, how to run and configure the app and its agent.

## How should I phrase my questions for the best answers?

Ask one thing at a time and include the name of the feature or topic, for example "How do I pay my SNGPL gas bill?" or "What is the Quick Loan limit?". Short, specific questions find the right entry more reliably than long questions that mix several topics. If an answer is close but not quite what you needed, ask a follow-up such as "and can I cancel it?"; the assistant uses your previous question to understand it.

## Can you give some example questions to try?

Try: "How do I send money to Easypaisa?", "Which bill providers are supported?", "What is the minimum order at Kolachi?", "How do I book a flight to Jeddah?", "What is Sadaqah Jariyah?", "How is Zakat calculated?", "Is the Private Safe really encrypted?", "Why did my transactions disappear?", "How do I change the language to Urdu?" and "How do I enable a local language model with Ollama?".

## Why does the assistant sometimes suggest related questions?

When the assistant quotes an FAQ entry, it also lists other entries that were close matches, so that you can explore nearby topics or find the right answer if the first one was not exactly what you meant. You can copy one of those questions into the chat box to see its full answer.

## Can the assistant answer questions about my own transactions?

No. The Help Center assistant answers general questions from this FAQ and does not read your transaction history. For a suggestion based on your own spending, use the Money Helper on the dashboard and press "Refresh Advice", which analyses your transactions on the server.

## Will the assistant's answers change over time?

Yes, whenever this FAQ is updated. Because the assistant retrieves its answers from the FAQ files each time the server starts, adding or correcting an entry immediately changes what the assistant says after the next restart, without any retraining. This keeps the assistant's answers consistent with the published FAQ document.
